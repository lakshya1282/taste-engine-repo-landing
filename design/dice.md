## Obeying the Dice: Rules & Commitments
1. **Seed:** Agrumea Farm (`agrumeafarm.it`) by Studio K95, Awwwards Site of the Day & Developer Award (Page 1, SOTD #4).
2. **Inherited Anchors (3 of 5):**
   - *Motion language:* Easing curves (`cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)`, `cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1)`, `cubic-bezier(.645, .045, .355, 1)`), fluid Lenis momentum, and layered stagger reveals.
   - *Color relationships:* Contrast ratios, dark-on-light paper logic, sharp border delineations, and high-contrast accenting.
   - *Interaction mechanics:* Reactive magnetic pills, responsive zone switches, physical toggles, and modal inspections.
3. **Background:** Cool light gray tone (`#eef2f6` / `#e8edf2`), tactile and crisp, never dark slop.
4. **Palette Logic:** Monochromatic cool slate/steel tones (`#11161d`, `#3b4654`, `#d1dbe6`, `#f8fafc`) with one electric hyper-orange (`#ff4500` / `#ff3b00`) from the opposite side of the cool wheel.
5. **Type Personality:** Raw handwritten/marker annotations (`Caveat` / `Covered By Your Grace` / hand-drawn SVG marker strokes) paired with ultra-clean, high-craft editorial sans (`Schibsted Grotesk` & `Spline Sans`).
6. **Layout Skeleton:** Spatial navigation between interactive rooms/zones with a fixed Spatial Radar HUD and zone sector jump mechanics.
7. **Motion Signature:** Interactive canvas/WebGL fluid distortion mesh & scroll velocity skew.
8. **Extra Constraint:** The logo is part of the interaction (clicking or dragging rolls interactive 3D dice and triggers the "Taste Engine Calibration" mode).
9. **Wildcard World:** Zine & risograph studio (print registration crosshairs `+`, micro halftone screens, raw stamp chips, editorial proof badges).
