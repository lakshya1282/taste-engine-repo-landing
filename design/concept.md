# Concept Document (Phase 5: Idea Burn)

## 5 Big Ideas Generated
1. *The AI Antidote Clinic:* A sterile clinical laboratory where generic websites are quarantined and diagnosed with "cliché symptoms". (BURNED — obvious metaphor, easily becomes cartoonish).
2. *The Taste Department:* A mid-century brutalist government agency issuing design compliance stamps and taste certifications. (BURNED — overly nostalgic, slow to navigate).
3. *The Risograph Print Workshop & Calibration Engine:* A tactile print studio where AI prompts are mechanically pulled across registration screens, ink beds, and calibration dice to produce one-of-a-kind prints.
4. *The Spatial Proving Ground / Radar Range:* A radar-mapped testing facility where agent code is stress-tested against design cliches across designated tactical zones.
5. *The Field Guide / Zine Dispatch:* An oversized unfolded print zine that maps out tactical coordinates, raw marker notes, and live interactive mechanical dials.

## Selection & Tiebreaker
Tiebreaker constraint from dice: **"The logo is part of the interaction"** combined with **"Map or spatial navigation between rooms/zones"** and **"Zine or risograph studio"**.

Selected Concept: **Idea 3 + Idea 4 Fusion: The Taste Calibration Studio & Zone Dispatch.**
The website is an interactive spatial studio and tactile dispatch bed. The logo is a living physical die: clicking it triggers a live roll that recalculates taste mutations on the fly. The entire site is organized into four designated spatial zones (The Deck, The Proving Grounds, The Spec Lab, The Dispatch Terminal) with an interactive spatial radar HUD that lets users jump between rooms or glide through them with smooth momentum.

## Core Sentence
> **This site is an interactive calibration workshop that uses physical randomness and print-bed craft to cure coding agents of generic design.**

## 3 Memorable Moments Visitors Will Screenshot or Record
1. **The Interactive Rollable Logo Die:** In the top header and hero, clicking the Taste Engine logo initiates a live 3D physics roll with sound-reactive haptic tick, rolling random design mutations (e.g., "Palette: Electric Vermilion on Steel", "Constraint: No Cards") in real-time.
2. **The Spatial Zone Radar HUD:** A floating tactile sector map in the bottom corner showing live coordinates (`ZONE 01: COMMAND DECK [34°N]`), visual sector blips, and one-click instant spatial glide navigation.
3. **The "Slop Autopsy" Interactive Slider & Before/After Proof Bed:** An interactive registration plate where users drag across a split screen showing generic "second-gen AI slop" (dark background, gold text, serif headline, fake counters) directly roasted and transformed into an authentic, high-craft editorial design.
