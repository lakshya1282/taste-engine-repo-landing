# Direction Card & Mutation Plan

## Direction Card
```text
CONCEPT: This site is an interactive calibration workshop that uses physical randomness and print-bed craft to cure coding agents of generic design.
WHY IT FITS THE AUDIENCE: Young vibe coders, developers, and designers who hate corporate SaaS fluff immediately recognize the raw craft, tactile feedback, and brutal honesty of a tool that proves its premise on its own page.
FEELING IN 3 SECONDS: "Whoever built this has authentic taste, unapologetic craft, and this thing will actually fix my agent."
SEED: https://www.agrumeafarm.it/ by Studio K95 (Awwwards SOTD & Developer Award) — credited as design inspiration.
ANCHORS INHERITED:
  1. Motion language: Precision non-standard cubic-bezier easings (cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1) and (.22, 1, .36, 1)), Lenis momentum scroll, and staggered typographic reveals.
  2. Color relationships: Clean light surface base contrasted against deep ink line structures and razor-sharp high-contrast accenting reserved exclusively for interactive elements.
  3. Interaction mechanics: Magnetic capsule pills, tactile state lifts, interactive spatial hover tracking, and clean modal inspections.
MUTATIONS APPLIED:
  - Background: Mutated from seed's warm peach/eggshell (#fdf3eb) to a cool light technical gray (#eef2f6 / #e6ebf0) with tactical risograph paper grain.
  - Palette: Mutated from citrus terracotta/blood orange to a sharp monochrome slate (#10141a, #333d4b, #cad5e2) with one loud unexpected accent from the opposite side of the wheel: high-voltage electric hyper-orange (#ff4500).
  - Typography: Replaced seed's IvyMode luxury serif display with expressive handwritten/marker annotations ("Caveat" / marker strokes) paired with ultra-clean, high-craft editorial grotesque ("Schibsted Grotesk" & "Spline Sans"). Banned all 26 generic AI fonts.
  - Layout Skeleton: Completely replaced standard e-commerce vertical scroll with a 4-Zone Spatial Navigation system (Command Deck, Proving Grounds, Spec Lab, Dispatch Terminal) with interactive Spatial HUD mini-radar.
  - Signature Motion: Layered an interactive WebGL / Canvas displacement ripple and velocity-based letterform skew over the seed's easing foundation.
  - Extra Constraint: The logo is an interactive rollable 3D die that visitors can click to test live mutation combinations.
SIGNATURE MOVES:
  - Layout: Spatial Zone Radar HUD in bottom corner that tracks current zone and allows instantaneous room-to-room spatial flights.
  - Type: High-impact editorial sans combined with raw handwritten marker callouts that circle and roast generic AI tropes.
  - Motion / Interaction: Live interactive 3D physics die roll in the header/hero, plus WebGL fluid canvas distortion trailing cursor and scroll velocity.
MEMORABLE MOMENTS:
  1. Clicking the logo die to roll real-time design constraints with tactile physics.
  2. The interactive "Slop Autopsy" before/after comparative inspection plate.
  3. The Spatial HUD Radar allowing visitors to teleport between pipeline rooms.
AVOIDING: Dark + gold palettes, generic purple gradient blobs, serif display + tiny mono tags, fake live counters, fake coordinates, fake customer logos, and cards with identical rounded corners.
```
